# Vehicle features and status

The model is under active development. The following reflects the current repository state rather than the complete real-vehicle specification.

## Implemented

- Renault Laguna II liftback body
- 1.6, 1.8, 2.0, 2.0 Turbo, 3.0 V6, 1.9 dCi, 2.2 dCi, and 2.0 dCi engines
- 5M, 6M, 4A, and 5A transmissions
- Engine-specific gearbox availability
- Open and limited-slip front differentials
- Standard, sport, and lowered suspension packages
- Spring-rate, damping, ride-height, and final-drive adjustments
- Standard and large brake options
- 15-inch steel and 17-inch alloy wheels
- Halogen and xenon headlights
- Factory and smoked tail lights
- Fog lights, parking sensors, wipers, mirrors, mouldings, badges, and plates
- Factory, privacy, and dark-film glass options
- Eleven era-appropriate paint names plus free paint selection
- Six generated `.pc` configurations
- Glow-map entries for major exterior lights

## Partially implemented

- Rear details and tailgate equipment
- Tyres and wheel choices
- Brake and suspension tuning
- Exterior damage and deformation
- Paint and trim options
- Role and tuning configurations

## Planned

- Estate and Phase 2 body variants
- Opening hood and tailgate
- Detailed dashboard and interior
- Keycard and start-button animation
- Shifter, pedal, seat, and gauge animations
- Engine sounds
- ABS and ESP controllers
- Breakable glass
- Detachable body panels
- Tow bars and cargo equipment
- Sunroof, roof rails, and roof racks
- Editable plate text
- Additional wheels and tyres
- Police, taxi, rally, and track packages

4. Steering direction
5. Wheel mesh placement
